A little about me

I started out at Thoughtworks, where nearly five years of consulting taught me to walk into an unfamiliar codebase, earn a team’s trust, and hand the system back in better shape than I found it. Today I’m a Senior Member of Technical Staff at Bluecopa, working on the backend behind finance and accounting data automation.

I’m happiest a layer or two below the surface — in the schema, the message broker, the migration that has to run while thousands of events keep flowing. I’ve built event-driven systems that move 10K+ events a day without losing one, and I treat a clean failure mode as part of the feature rather than an afterthought.

Mostly I work in Java, Kotlin, and Python across AWS and GCP. I care just as much about the craft around the code: tests that genuinely exercise the domain, observability you can actually reason with at 2am, and designs the next engineer can follow without a map.

Lately a growing share of that work is LLM-powered — building AI agents, the tool-calling that lets them act, and the evaluation and guardrails that keep them honest. It’s the same instinct as everything else I build: make it capable, then make it fail safely.
